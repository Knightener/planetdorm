About PlanetDorm

We made PlanetDorm for UMD students just like you! We wanted a way for students to leave reviews for University of Maryland dorms, so that incoming and returning students would know exactly what it's like to live there before they actually move in. what began as a simple idea sprouted into a feature-packed website designed to simplify the dorm locating process. We hope you find our website useful!

Future updates:

We plan to add many more features in the future, such as off-campus housing, a suggestion submission feature, google maps reviews integration, and more enhanced descriptions on dorm types.
