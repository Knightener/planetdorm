PlanetDorm

What is PlanetDorm?

PlanetDorm is the only website featuring UMD dorms. We made PlanetDorm for UMD students just like you! We found there were almost no reviews for dorms at UMD and we wanted to change that, so that incoming and returning students would know exactly what it's like to live there before they actually move in. Even if you were already assigned a dorm, you can learn more about your future home right here on Planet Dorm! We encourage everyone who has spent a few months or years in a dorm to leave a review. Also, if you have any questions check out the FAQ or send us a message. We hope you enjoy our site!

Our Story

Planet Dorm began as a simple idea. Me and my friends just got our dorm assignment and we were excited to see where we would spend our first year at. We quickly ran into a problem. We could not find anything about our dorm aside from a few old reviews. Fast forward to a few months later, we had the same problem. However, this time we were actually deciding on a dorm and we weren't auto assigned. That's when we decided to build a platform where students like us could share their experiences and first-time university students can remain excited about their future home!

Future updates

We plan to add many more features in the future, such as off-campus housing, a suggestion submission feature, Google Maps reviews integration, and more enhanced descriptions on dorm types.
