PlanetDorm

Made by Terps, for Terps.

We made PlanetDorm for UMD students just like you! We found there weren't a lot of reviews for UMD dorms and we wanted to change that, so that incoming and returning students would know exactly what it's like to live there before they actually move in. What began as a simple idea sprouted into a feature-packed website designed to simplify the dorm locating process. We hope you find our website useful!

Future updates

We plan to add many more features in the future, such as off-campus housing, a suggestion submission feature, Google Maps reviews integration, and more enhanced descriptions on dorm types.
